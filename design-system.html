Voz SABER

“Not sure which program fits? Let's explore your options.”

Cercanía sin presión. Una invitación clara y una persona al otro lado.

Control editorial

Promesas que necesitan evidencia.

“Guaranteed job.” / “Everyone qualifies.”

Evitar garantías de admisión, empleo o ayuda financiera. Explicar el proceso, los requisitos y el apoyo disponible.

Disclosure pattern

Complex information can still feel simple.

Program name, credential, format, essential duration information and the most relevant next action should be visible before users expand additional details.
Use the official accreditor name, explain whether the status is institutional or programmatic, and connect the statement to a current official source.
Use a document card when the file has continuing value, and display its title, type and date so students understand what they are opening.
