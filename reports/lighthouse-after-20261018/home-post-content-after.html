Career Outlook For Registered Nurses

Registered nurses are the backbone of the healthcare industry. With a growing population, increasing rates of chronic conditions, and a greater need for preventative care, the demand for registered nurses will only increase.

According to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, the median annual wage for registered nurses was $77,600 in May 2021 and is expected to grow.

Over the next decade, registered nurse employment is projected to grow 9 percent from 2020 to 2030, about as fast as the average for all occupations. About 194,500 openings for registered nurses are projected each year, on average, over the decade. Many of those openings are expected to result from the need to replace workers who transfer to different occupations or exit the labor force, such as retiring.

FAQs

We Have Answers To Your Questions!

Is Financial Aid available for the Physical Therapist Assistant Program?

Yes, Financial Aid is available if you qualify. Our friendly staff is ready to help you.

Do you have to go to medical school to be a Physical Therapist Assistant (PTA)?

You do not have to go to medical school to become a PTA. But you should check that the school you choose is licensed by the State Department of Education and accredited by the Commission on Accreditation in Physical Therapy Education (CAPTE).

Can I become a Physical Therapy Assistant (PTA) right out of high school?

You may study to become a PTA right out of high school.

Why should I choose to become a Physical Therapist Assistant? (PTA)

Suppose you’re not sure but are looking for something flexible and autonomous. In that case, you should consider the many benefits that a career as a PTA could bring, including that you can help people regain their quality of life. Also, the Miami-Dade county means average salary for a Physical Therapist Assistant is $67,220, with a mean hourly wage of $32.32. O*NET Online lists Physical Therapist Assistant as a job with a Bright Outlook, meaning Bright Outlook occupations are expected to grow rapidly in the next several years. They may have large numbers of job openings or are new and emerging occupations.

How do I get a Professional Nursing Degree?

If you are attending school in Florida, you must attend a school that is licensed by the Florida Department of Education to offer Associate Degree Programs, specifically Professional Nursing Programs and you should check that the school is licensed by the state’s Board of Nursing.

Can I take the NCLEX without a nursing degree?

To take the NCLEX-RN, you must be a nursing program graduate.

Can I become an RN in 2 years?

Yes, provided you do not fail any courses, you may graduate in 24 months. The 24 months include the general education and core courses required to become a nurse graduate.
