Click the image to download the form To initiate the process, simply make a payment by calling 305-443-9170 at the main office. (Please mention the reference “payment for a transcript request”). The fee is $10.00 per copy and $25.00 for out-of-state requests. We’ve made it as convenient as possible for you. Download and complete the request form with your information and scan the form to the following email address: registrar@sabercollege.edu (All Programs) linette@sabercollege.edu (All Nursing after Dec 2020) destevez@sabercollege.edu (All Nursing before Dec 2020) lalvarez@sabercollege.edu (All Nursing before Dec 2020) karocha@sabercollege.edu (PTA Program) Don’t forget to attach proof of payment. Transcripts will be prepared for pickup every Friday afternoon before 2:00 PM. If the request is to be sent to other institutions, this will occur every Friday by the close of business.
