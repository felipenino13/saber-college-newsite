In accordance with U.S. Department of Education regulations, institutions like SABER College are required to disclose specific facts about these programs to help prospective students make informed decisions about their education and career path. These disclosures include details such as program duration, occupational outcomes, typical student loan debt, and graduation or job placement data. They are intended to ensure that students understand both the academic commitment and career possibilities associated with each program before enrolling. Under 34 CFR § 668.6, the federal government requires institutions of higher education to share program-level facts for all eligible gainful employment programs. These disclosures provide answers to key questions such as: How long does the program take to complete? What types of careers can I pursue upon graduation? What is the typical loan debt for program graduates? What are the job placement and completion outcomes? This level of transparency empowers students to compare similar programs across colleges and choose the option that best fits their personal goals and financial situation. Gainful Employment Programs at SABER College The following Associate of Science degree programs at SABER College are designated as gainful employment programs: 1. Associate of Science – Physical Therapist Assistant (PTA) This program prepares students to assist licensed physical therapists in delivering rehabilitative care. Graduates are equipped to work in hospitals, outpatient centers, and long-term care facilities. 2. Associate of Science – Professional Nursing Program (PNP) SABER College’s Nursing program prepares students to sit for the NCLEX-RN and begin professional practice as Registered Nurses. Graduates work in a wide range of healthcare environments including hospitals, clinics, and community care centers. Program Disclosure Information Fiscal Year: 2026-2027 In compliance with 34 CFR § 668.6(b), SABER College provides the following information for prospective students: Program Length Physical Therapist Assistant Program: Approximately 20 months Professional Nursing Program: Approximately 22 months Tuition and Fees Tuition and institutional fee details are available through our Financial Aid Office. SABER College does not post tuition online but will provide a personalized cost breakdown during your admissions process. Books and Materials Estimated costs for required books, uniforms, and learning resources are shared during advising sessions and financial aid counseling. Median Loan Debt The median loan debt incurred by students who completed these programs is disclosed during one-on-one financial aid sessions. Students are guided through financial literacy resources to ensure informed borrowing decisions. Job Placement and Completion Rates Placement and completion data are reported annually to accreditors and regulatory agencies. This information is made available to prospective students upon request via the Admissions Office.
