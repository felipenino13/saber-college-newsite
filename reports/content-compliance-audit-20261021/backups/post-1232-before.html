2. Information We Collect

We collect information from you in several ways — both directly and automatically — to deliver requested information and improve our services.

a. Information You Provide Directly

When you complete a request form on our website, through Facebook, Instagram, Google Ads, or via a dedicated landing page, you may provide:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Program of interest
  • Preferred class schedule (morning or evening)
  • Preferred start date or language preference
  • Any additional details voluntarily submitted in a comment or message

This information allows SABER College to respond accurately to your inquiry and connect you with the appropriate admissions representative.

b. Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our site or a campaign landing page, we may automatically collect:

  • Device type, browser version, and IP address
  • Date/time of visits and pages viewed
  • General location (approximate region) for analytics and security
  • Cookie and tracking data to enhance site performance

c. Comments and Uploaded Media

  • If you leave a comment, we collect the data shown in the form, your IP address, and browser agent string to detect spam.
  • If you upload photos, please avoid embedding location (EXIF GPS) data, as visitors can download and extract it.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely for legitimate educational and marketing purposes, including to:

  • Contact you about SABER College programs and admissions.
  • Respond to questions and send requested materials.
  • Schedule a campus visit or virtual appointment.
  • Send reminders and follow-ups about enrollment opportunities.
  • Improve our website experience and advertising effectiveness.

We may reach you by phone call, SMS/text message, WhatsApp, email, Meta Direct Message (Facebook or Instagram), or by replying to a comment you post on our official social pages.

Your information will never be sold, rented, or shared for unrelated marketing purposes.

4. Advertising and Lead Generation Compliance

SABER College uses advertising and lead-generation platforms such as Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google Ads, and dedicated landing pages hosted on sabercollege.edu or on external domains managed by the College or its authorized agencies.

When you submit your information through a form or landing page:

  • You authorize SABER College and its representatives to contact you using the methods above regarding its educational programs.
  • Your data is securely transmitted to our Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system and used exclusively for admissions-related communications.
  • Your consent remains in place until you revoke it, which you may do at any time by contacting us.
  • Each form or landing page contains a link to this Privacy Policy and describes the specific offer or program being promoted.

SABER College complies with:

  • Meta Lead Ad Terms (Oct 2025)
  • Meta Advertising Standards
  • Google Ads Data Use and Consent Policy
  • U.S. Telecommunications Consumer Protection Act (TCPA)
  • CAN-SPAM Act and applicable data privacy laws
