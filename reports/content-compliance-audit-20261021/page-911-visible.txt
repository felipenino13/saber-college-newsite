Understanding tuition, fees, and potential financial aid is one of the most important steps in planning for your education. SABER College is committed to transparency and accessibility, providing this federally required tool to support informed financial decision-making. This calculator offers an early look at your estimated net cost—the difference between the total cost of attendance and any non-loan financial aid you may be eligible to receive. Using historical data and the information you provide, the tool delivers a personalized estimate tailored to your unique circumstances. While it does not replace the official financial aid process, it serves as a valuable resource for financial planning. What Is the Net Price Calculator? The Net Price Calculator is designed for undergraduate students exploring enrollment at SABER College. It allows you to estimate your likely education costs after grant and scholarship aid, based on a combination of federal formulas and previous year institutional data. Though not an official financial offer, the results offer insight into what similar students paid in prior years—giving you a better sense of affordability before you complete your FAFSA or submit an application. This is just one of the ways SABER College supports students in preparing for higher education. Why Use the Net Price Calculator? Selecting the right college is a significant decision—both personally and financially. The Net Price Calculator gives you the clarity needed to: Understand possible out-of-pocket expenses Preview the impact of financial aid packages Begin planning realistically for your educational investment This tool is particularly useful for those pursuing Gainful Employment programs at SABER College, including our Nursing and Physical Therapist Assistant programs. Whether you’re a recent high school graduate or a working adult returning to school, this resource helps you make a more confident and informed choice. How to Use It Using the calculator is quick and straightforward. In about 5–10 minutes, you’ll answer a few basic questions about income, household size, and dependency status. Based on your answers, the tool will generate an estimate of your net price—the cost of attendance minus average financial aid. Keep in mind, the more accurate the information you enter, the more closely your estimate will reflect your potential financial aid eligibility. While the calculator cannot predict every detail, it provides a useful baseline for budgeting and comparison. Need Help? If you need help understanding your results or want to speak with someone about your options, SABER College offers support through our Financial Aid Office. Our experienced staff can guide you through the FAFSA, help you understand financial aid eligibility, and answer questions related to tuition and payment planning.
