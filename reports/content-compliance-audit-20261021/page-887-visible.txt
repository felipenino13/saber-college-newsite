Located in Miami, Florida, SABER College operates as a not-for-profit institution under 501(c)(3) status. Its mission centers on expanding access to education, particularly in the healthcare field, while supporting students through quality instruction, experienced faculty, and personalized support services. SABER College is licensed by the Florida Department of Education Commission for Independent Education (CIE). This licensure confirms the college’s authorization to operate as a postsecondary institution in the State of Florida and ensures that its academic programs, instructional staff, facilities, and institutional policies meet the required state guidelines. Being licensed by CIE provides transparency, accountability, and credibility for students and families seeking a trusted educational institution. In addition to state licensure, SABER College Accreditation includes institutional accreditation from the Council on Occupational Education (COE). COE is a national accrediting agency recognized by the U.S. Department of Education and operates under the Higher Education Act of 1965. This accreditation validates the overall quality of SABER College’s academic programs, governance, student services, learning outcomes, and financial practices. Accreditation from COE assures students that SABER College operates with integrity and adheres to high academic and professional standards. For more information about COE, please visit www.council.org. SABER College also holds programmatic accreditation for its Physical Therapist Assistant (PTA) Program through the Commission on Accreditation in Physical Therapy Education (CAPTE). This accreditation ensures that the PTA program is aligned with the standards required to train competent, ethical, and skilled physical therapist assistants. CAPTE is the only accrediting agency recognized by the U.S. Department of Education for physical therapy education. Its rigorous review process evaluates program content, faculty qualifications, clinical experiences, student outcomes, and institutional support. CAPTE is located at 3030 Potomac Avenue, Suite 100, Alexandria, Virginia 22305-3085. For inquiries, CAPTE may be contacted by phone at 703-706-3245, by email at accreditation@apta.org, or by visiting www.capteonline.org. Individuals who wish to file a formal complaint about the PTA program are encouraged to follow SABER College’s internal grievance policy and due process procedures first. If further action is needed, CAPTE may be contacted using the information provided above. To reach the program or institution directly, call 305-443-9170 or email karocha@sabercollege.edu. Another important element of SABER College Accreditation is its designation as a Title IV eligible institution by the U.S. Department of Education. This approval allows qualified students to apply for federal financial aid, including Pell Grants and student loans. Participation in Title IV programs reflects the institution’s compliance with federal financial aid regulations and enhances access to postsecondary education for students who require financial assistance to pursue their academic and professional goals. SABER College is also approved to offer educational services through Vocational Rehabilitation programs. These programs provide specialized support for students with disabilities or other qualifying conditions, ensuring they receive the guidance, resources, and accommodations needed to succeed in their chosen field. This approval further strengthens the college’s ability to serve a diverse student population and expand pathways to employment for individuals facing additional challenges. SABER College Accreditation plays a central role in building confidence among students, families, employers, and regulators. It reflects the college’s dedication to educational quality, student support, and continuous improvement. Students enrolled in Nursing or PTA programs can expect a learning environment that meets or exceeds the standards set by accrediting agencies and professional organizations. To learn more about SABER College Accreditation, available programs, or the admissions process, we invite you to contact our team directly.
