The program helps ensure that United States schools comply with the full range of requirements for international students. To be a SEVP-certified school, we must undergo rigorous oversight of all aspects of the program, including immigration compliance and international student services. This means that our school is approved to enroll students with an F-1 student visa and M-1 international students with an I-20, which is a document they need to apply for a student visa. I-20 Process - International student services As an international student, you’ll need to complete the following steps: Complete all academic requirements for admission to a degree program. This step is handled exclusively by the SABER Admissions Office for students pursuing an associate’s degree program or general education classes. Prepare proof of financial support. The International Student Services office will need your I-20 Form, which lists your financial resources and course of study. The SABER Admissions Office will issue this form. As part of your I-20 application, you must submit a completed affidavit of support form with a bank signature or bank letter detailing funding, a government sponsorship letter, or any combination thereof to your I-20 application.Note: Government sponsorship letters must specifically mention SABER College as the designated school for the student, and government sponsorship letters must cover one full year (12 months) of student costs if used as the sole source of funding. To provide you with your I-20, SABER’s International Student Services department will need a scanned copy of your passport’s data page. This should include a clear image of your name, date of birth, and passport expiration date. Note that F-1 visa holders must maintain a passport that is at least 6 months from expiration while inside the United States. If you are bringing any of your underage children or spouse to the U.S. with you, we need to know so that we can add them to your I-20 application as F-2 dependents. If your dependents are arriving in the U.S. after you have received your I-20, please you will need to complete the “Add New Dependent” form instead. Once you have completed all five steps and submitted them to our office, we will begin processing your I-20. You will receive an e-mail message asking you to confirm that all the data on your I-20 is accurate. Be sure to double-check details such as the spelling of your name and the accuracy of your date of birth. You will also be required to provide information for shipping your form I-20.
